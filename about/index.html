Our mission

Commission on Logic Education

The Commission on Logic Education (CLE) is part of the Division for Logic, Methodology and Philosophy of Science and Technology (DLMPST) of the International Union of History and Philosophy of Science and Technology (IUHPST).

The Commission’s objective is to promote and facilitate the introduction and development of education in logic, broadly conceived, at schools and universities worldwide, as well as in other forms of education.

The Commission strives to implement the vision of the role and importance of logic education expressed in the 2015 Helsinki Manifesto of the DLMPST. It acts through networks of national and local representatives, alongside academic and other organizations concerned with logic and education.
